Transparent Project Workflow

Our streamlined 4-stage process ensures elite quality from conception to anchoring.

Stage 1: 40% Mobilization

Contract execution, conceptual sign-off, and procurement of specialized resins, silicon, and structural armatures.

Stage 2: 30% Sculpt & Casting

Primary carving, hardcoating, and creation of master molds. The physical form is brought to life in the studio.

Stage 3: 20% Inspection

Pre-dispatch quality control, patina/paint finishing, and client review to ensure the piece meets our elite standards.

Stage 4: 10% Installation

Secure transport, on-site structural anchoring, and final environmental blending at your resort or commercial space anywhere in the PH.
